# Questions That Count — Part B: Your module

### B1. Module and outcomes

- Module: Physical Science Foundations (PHY101)
- Level of study: Undergraduate Y1
- Learning outcomes, each with the level it targets:

| Outcome | Target level |
| --- | --- |
| Explain why water boils at a lower temperature at altitude | Understand |
| Predict how changing pressure changes a liquid's boiling point | Apply |
| Interpret a phase diagram to identify the state of a substance | Analyse |

### B2. What was taught

- Content source: Week 1–3 slides and notes
- Cases, examples and data used in teaching:
  - Cooking pasta at the top of Ben Nevis
  - Pressure cooker in a sea-level kitchen
  - Phase diagram of water from the Week 3 slides

### B3. What students get wrong

| Misconception |
| --- |
| 100 °C is a fixed property of water |
| Higher means colder, so lower boiling point |
| Bubbles in boiling water are air, not water vapour |
